The Agenda · First 100 Days

The Seven Pillars
of A Better Abuja

Not promises. LEGISLATION. Seven bills I will introduce in my first 100 days as FCT Senator.

🛡️ Security 💼 Economy 🏗️ Infrastructure 📚 Education 👶 Maternal Health 🏘️ Land Rights 👁️ Transparency
🛡️
01 / SECURITY

Nobody Should Fear For Their Life In Abuja

"Safe streets, community policing, and the freedom to come home at night without fear."

📋 Key Legislation: Abuja Residents Security Bill

The Problem

  • Private security for the rich, "one chance" for the rest
  • Politicians travel in armored convoys while you fear for your life
  • Areas like Nyanya, Karu, Kubwa unsafe after 8 PM
  • Kidnappings go unreported to "protect Abuja's image"
  • Security budget spent on VIP protection, not community safety

The Solutions

  • Community policing in EVERY neighborhood
  • Solar streetlights in high-risk areas (Mabushi, Galadimawa, Area 1&2)
  • Quick-response security teams within 5 minutes
  • Partnership with local vigilantes and traditional rulers
  • Technology-driven crime prevention and tracking

Timeline & Accountability

  • Community policing framework: Days 1–30
  • Solar streetlight installation begins: Month 2
  • Rapid response units operational: Month 3
  • Technology crime tracking platform: Month 6
  • Monthly security town halls in all 6 councils
🎯 Expected Impact: 70% reduction in "one chance" incidents, 24/7 safe streets across all 6 area councils
💼
02 / ECONOMIC EMPOWERMENT

Your Hustle Must Pay. Your Sweat Must Count.

"Jobs, fair wages, rent control, and an economy that works for working people."

📋 Key Legislation: FCT Economic Empowerment & Rent Control Bill

The Problem

  • You earn ₦300K/month. Rent takes ₦150K. Transport ₦50K. Food ₦80K. Nothing left.
  • Landlords increase rent 50% yearly with no regulation
  • Government contracts go to connected elites only
  • Youth unemployment ignored while they send kids abroad

The Solutions

  • Rent Control Bill capping annual increases at 10%
  • Mandatory quotas for women & youth-owned businesses in government contracts
  • 100,000 youth trained in ICT, coding, agribusiness, vocational skills
  • 10,000 micro-loans for small businesses (₦500K–₦2M)
  • Low-Cost Housing Fund with affordable mortgages

Expected Outcomes

  • 100,000+ jobs created across FCT
  • Rent becomes affordable again
  • 10,000 new businesses supported
  • Women & youth get 30% of govt contract value
  • Micro-loan access for informal sector workers
🎯 Expected Impact: 100,000+ jobs created, rent affordable for middle and lower-income residents
🏗️
03 / INFRASTRUCTURE

Water. Roads. Light. Healthcare. These Aren't Favors — They're Your Rights.

"The basics aren't luxuries — they're rights you've already paid for."

The Problem

  • They have private boreholes. You queue for water trucks.
  • Their estates have perfect roads. Yours are death traps.
  • Kwali, Abaji, Kuje get nothing while billions are "allocated"
  • Healthcare centers without doctors, drugs, or equipment

The Solutions

  • 500,000+ residents getting clean water via solar-powered boreholes
  • Major road rehabilitation in Abaji, Kwali, Bwari, Kuje
  • 50+ Primary Healthcare Centers fully equipped and operational
  • Light Rail expansion to Kubwa, Nyanya, Karu, Lugbe
  • Every budget line item published and tracked in real-time

Priority Projects

  • Solar boreholes: 200 communities in Year 1
  • Kubwa–City Centre Light Rail corridor
  • Road upgrade: Abaji–Gwagwalada highway
  • 50 PHCs: staff, equipment, drugs, 24/7 operation
  • Real-time infrastructure tracker launched Day 1
🎯 Expected Impact: Every area council gets functional water, roads, and healthcare. No more "some are more equal" infrastructure.
📚
04 / EDUCATION

No Child Left Behind

"Quality education for every child, whether you live in Maitama or Kwali."

The Problem

  • Their children attend ₦5M/year schools abroad
  • Your children study under trees, on broken desks
  • Teachers unpaid for months while budgets "disappear"
  • Public schools in Kwali, Kuje have no roofs, no books, no labs

The Solutions

  • 200+ public schools renovated with classrooms, libraries, science labs
  • 50,000 scholarships covering tuition, WAEC, NECO, JAMB fees
  • Competitive teacher salaries to attract quality educators
  • Free ICT training centers in AMAC, Bwari, Gwagwalada
  • 10,000 youth trained in digital and coding skills

Accountability Measures

  • School renovation tracker: every school, every naira, publicly visible
  • Teacher salary payment verified monthly
  • Student outcomes measured annually
  • Parent School Monitoring Committees in every school
  • Annual education town halls per area council
🎯 Expected Impact: Every child gets quality education regardless of parents' income or location. No more Maitama vs Kwali education inequality.
👶
05 / MATERNAL HEALTH

Pregnancy Is Not A Death Sentence

"Every woman can deliver safely, with dignity, without fear."

The Problem

  • Their wives deliver in private hospitals with celebrity doctors
  • Your wife might die because she can't afford ₦50,000
  • Nigeria has one of the world's highest maternal mortality rates
  • Women in Kwali, Abaji deliver at home because clinics are too far

The Solutions

  • Free Maternal Health Bill: comprehensive prenatal, delivery, postnatal care
  • Mobile clinics reaching every rural community monthly
  • 100,000+ women receiving maternal health services
  • Emergency obstetric care at every PHC, 24/7
  • Maternal waiting homes for high-risk pregnancies in remote areas

First 100 Days

  • Introduce Free Maternal Health Bill in Senate
  • Deploy 10 mobile clinics to most underserved wards
  • Audit all 50+ PHCs for obstetric readiness
  • Partner with private hospitals for emergency referrals
  • Launch maternal health hotline for rural women
🎯 Expected Impact: 80% reduction in maternal mortality. No woman in Abuja dies giving life because she couldn't afford care.
🏘️
06 / LAND RIGHTS

They Can't Just Bulldoze Your Home

"Land rights, fair compensation, and housing you can actually afford."

The Problem

  • They bulldoze your home overnight without notice
  • Gbagyi, Koro, Bassa, Gade people displaced, zero compensation
  • Land grabbed for "development" but indigenous owners get nothing
  • You're told to "go to court" — knowing you can't afford it

The Solutions

  • Indigenous Rights & Land Protection Bill with FULL compensation
  • Land Rights Tribunal with indigenous representation
  • Mandatory notice period, fair market value compensation, resettlement
  • Rent Control Bill protecting tenants from arbitrary evictions
  • Affordable housing programs targeting low & middle-income residents

Protection Mechanisms

  • No demolition without 90-day advance notice
  • Fair market compensation BEFORE any displacement
  • Free legal aid for land dispute cases
  • Indigenous communities get seats on land use decisions
  • Transparent land registry accessible to all citizens
🎯 Expected Impact: Justice for indigenous people, housing security for all, zero illegal demolitions without due process and compensation.
👁️
07 / TRANSPARENCY

You Will See Where Every Kobo Goes

"Accountability, open budgets, and a government that answers to YOU."

The Problem

  • Budgets "allocated" but nobody sees where money goes
  • 10% revenue for area councils — never reaches them
  • Contracts awarded to cronies at inflated prices
  • Projects "completed" on paper, abandoned in reality

The Solutions

  • FCTA Accountability Bill: Every budget public, every contract tracked
  • Monthly town halls in all 6 area councils — minister MUST attend
  • Real-time project tracking via digital platform
  • Community monitoring committees for every major project
  • Open budget platform: See where EVERY kobo goes

Accountability Systems

  • Live budget dashboard — updated daily
  • Contract award notifications to citizens
  • Project completion verification by citizens
  • Anonymous whistleblower system with legal protection
  • Annual public audit presentation in plain language
🎯 Expected Impact: Complete transparency, 100% accountability. Corruption has nowhere to hide when citizens can see everything in real time.
