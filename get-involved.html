Become a Volunteer
Join the 100,000-strong army

We're building an army across all 62 wards of the FCT. From knocking on doors to registering voters, from organizing town halls to spreading the message — we need you.

  • Door-to-door canvassing in your ward
  • Voter registration drives
  • Social media amplification
  • Poll monitoring on election day
  • Weekend Warrior (2-4 hrs) or Full-Time Champion (10+ hrs)
Sign Up as Volunteer
Ward Champion
Lead your community

Ward Champions are the backbone of our grassroots strategy. Lead volunteer teams in your ward, organize local events, and bridge campaign to community.

  • Recruit & coordinate 10-20 ward volunteers
  • Organize weekly meetings
  • Primary contact for residents in your ward
  • 5+ hours/week commitment
  • Must be a resident of your ward
Apply as Ward Champion
Host a House Party
Bring the movement home

Invite 10–20 friends, neighbors, or colleagues to your home for an informal A Better Abuja conversation. One of the most effective grassroots tools we have.

  • We provide all campaign materials
  • Video content featuring Aisha
  • Snacks budget support for parties of 15+
  • Register your date and get materials in 3 days

Use your professional skills

  • Communications: writing, media, press
  • Logistics: events, transport, coordination
