Area Council 01
AMAC
Abuja Municipal
1.8M Residents 11 Wards Most Populated

The Problems Now

  • 3-hour daily commutes from Nyanya to city centre
  • Rent in Kubwa now costs what Wuse cost 5 years ago
  • "One chance" robberies rampant on Nyanya-Karu, Kubwa, Lugbe routes
  • Public hospitals overwhelmed, schools overcrowded
  • Water supply erratic in satellite areas like Karshi, Orozo

A Better Abuja Solutions

Security

Community policing + CCTV on Nyanya-Karu, Kubwa Expressway. Solar streetlights. Rapid response units.

→ 70% fewer "one chance" incidents
Infrastructure

Light Rail expansion to Nyanya, Karu, Lugbe, Kubwa. Road rehabilitation. 150,000+ with clean water.

→ Commute times cut 50%
Economy

Rent Control Bill capping increases at 10%. 15,000 scholarships. 20,000 youth trained in digital skills.

→ 15,000+ jobs created
800K+Residents Impacted

50%Fewer Crime Incidents
40%Drop in Rent Hikes
Area Council 02
Bwari
Urban & Rural
350K Residents 10 Wards Water Crisis
"Women in Kubwa Extension, Ushafa, Dutse walk over a kilometre for water every single day. This is Nigeria's capital city."

The Problems Now

  • No piped water in most communities — women walk 1+ km daily
  • Roads in Ushafa, Dutse impassable during rainy season
  • Bandits operating in rural communities
  • Hospitals lack basic equipment, rural areas have zero healthcare
  • Maternal deaths from preventable causes

A Better Abuja Solutions

Water (Priority #1)

30+ solar-powered boreholes. 100,000+ residents with clean water for the first time.

→ Clean water within 200m of every home
Roads

Complete rehabilitation of Kubwa, Dutse, Ushafa, Dutse-Alhaji roads. Drainage to prevent flooding.

→ Passable roads year-round
Land Rights

Protection for indigenous Gbagyi communities. Fair compensation. Land Rights Tribunal.

→ Justice for indigenous people

Area Council 03
Gwagwalada
University Town
300K Residents 10 Wards Healthcare Crisis

The Problems Now

  • Gwagwalada Main Hospital overwhelmed, no functional PHCs in rural areas
  • Women die in childbirth from preventable causes
  • Roads to surrounding villages are death traps
  • Most villages have no clean water
  • Sky-high youth unemployment despite university proximity

A Better Abuja Solutions

Healthcare (Priority #1)

10+ PHCs equipped and operational. Mobile clinics weekly. Free maternal care for 25,000+ women. University of Abuja Teaching Hospital partnership.

→ 70% reduction in maternal deaths
Youth Employment

15,000 youth trained via university partnerships. 3,000 micro-loans. University-community entrepreneurship programs.

→ 12,000+ jobs, youth unemployment halved
Infrastructure

Major road rehabilitation. 40+ boreholes. 80,000+ residents with clean water.

→ All-season access, clean water for all

Area Council 04
Kuje
Agricultural Potential
250K Residents 10 Wards Security Focus

The Problems Now

  • Security anxiety from Kuje Prison proximity; communities feel unsafe
  • Bad roads isolate communities from markets and services
  • No clean water in most villages
  • Healthcare non-existent in rural areas
  • Agricultural potential unrealized — crops spoil, youth leave

A Better Abuja Solutions

Security (Priority #1)

Enhanced security protocols around prison area. Community policing all 10 wards. Solar streetlights. Rapid response teams.

→ 60% drop in incidents, 40% improved perception
Agricultural Economy

Farm-to-market roads, equipment support, modern farming training for 12,000 youth. Market infrastructure.

→ Agricultural productivity doubles, 8,000+ jobs
Infrastructure

Complete road network rehabilitation. 35+ boreholes. 70,000+ residents with clean water. 8+ PHCs fully operational.

→ All communities accessible year-round

Area Council 05 — The Forgotten One
Kwali
Gbagyi Homeland
200K Residents 11 Wards Most Neglected
"Abuja's capital sits on Gbagyi land. Yet Kwali gets nothing. This ends in 2027."

The Problems Now

  • Most neglected council — completely forgotten by government
  • Women walk 2+ km daily for water; children miss school to fetch water
  • No functional healthcare; women die in childbirth
  • Roads literally don't exist in most areas — communities cut off
  • Indigenous Gbagyi displaced without compensation

A Better Abuja Solutions

Emergency Water (Priority #1)

50+ solar-powered boreholes — highest density in FCT. 60,000+ residents with clean water FOR THE FIRST TIME.

→ Basic dignity restored
Land Rights — Justice At Last

Full compensation for ALL Gbagyi, Koro, Bassa, Gade land taken. Land Rights Tribunal with indigenous representation.

→ Historical injustice finally addressed
Roads Built From Scratch

Not rehabilitation — actual road construction. Communities connected. Isolation ended.

→ Kwali joins A Better Abuja

Area Council 06 — The Most Remote
Abaji
FCT's Future Food Basket
180K Residents 10 Wards Ending Isolation
"Abaji is 2 hours from Abuja but might as well be in another country. Your distance will become your advantage — as FCT's agricultural powerhouse."

The Problems Now

  • 2 hours from Abuja, completely cut off — no roads, no infrastructure
  • Rich farmland but no support — crops spoil, potential wasted
  • No water. No healthcare. No functional schools.
  • Youth exodus — leaving for cities; communities dying slowly

A Better Abuja Solutions

End Isolation (Priority #1)

Major road construction linking Abaji to Abuja. Agricultural transport routes. 50,000+ with clean water.

→ Isolation ends permanently
Agricultural Revolution

Modern equipment, cold storage, farm-to-market roads. 8,000 farmers trained. Cooperative formation support.

→ Agricultural productivity triples
Healthcare & Education

6+ PHCs built from scratch. Mobile clinics weekly. 20+ schools renovated. 4,000 scholarships.

→ Services where none existed before
