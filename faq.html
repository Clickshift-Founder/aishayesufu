👤About Aisha

Let me be clear: Abuja is not just for indigenes. Abuja is Nigeria's capital — YOUR capital, OUR capital.

The Igbo woman selling in Wuse Market — she's Abuja. The Hausa okada rider in Kubwa — he's Abuja. The Yoruba civil servant in Garki — she's Abuja. The Gbagyi farmer whose land was taken — he's Abuja.

This question about indigeneship is the exact distraction they want you focused on while they steal from all of us.

I'm asking you to choose COMPETENCE over tribe, INTEGRITY over ethnicity, RESULTS over region.

My agenda puts indigenous rights at the CENTER — full compensation for ancestral lands, legal protection against displacement, a Land Rights Tribunal. Show me ONE so-called 'indigene' politician who has fought for indigenous people the way I'm committed to fighting.

You know what that criticism really means? It means "She can't be controlled." "She won't play the game." "She'll actually do what she promised."

Let me ask you something: How's it working out electing politicians who've NEVER fought for anything in their lives?

What activists know how to do: FIGHT. We know how to mobilize people. We know how to build movements. We know how to take on the powerful and WIN.

Track record speaks: Co-convened #BringBackOurGirls and kept it alive for 1,000+ days. Led #NigeriansMarchAgainstCorruption. Stood in #EndSARS when it was dangerous. Founded Citizens Hub, empowering thousands.

The best leaders didn't come from political dynasties. They came from the struggle: Nelson Mandela, Václav Havel, Wangari Maathai.

Let me be very clear: I am not running AGAINST Senator Ireti Kingibe. I am running to REPRESENT the hopes and aspirations of Abuja citizens.

Senator Ireti is a colleague, a woman I respect. Yes, Peter Obi is my principal. And here's what's important: Peter Obi supports BOTH of us. Because democracy is strengthened when citizens have OPTIONS.

Democracy thrives on choice, not coronation.

This is not about conflict between two women. This is about giving the people of Abuja the power to choose their own representation based on who they believe will fight hardest for them. Let the voters decide.

I didn't leave the ADC because of disappointment or conflict. I left to honor a commitment and follow where the movement is going.

When I joined the ADC, it was to support Peter Obi's presidential ambition. After the 2023 elections, as we looked ahead to 2027, Peter Obi chose the NDC as the vehicle for this vision.

My decision to join the NDC wasn't about hero worship — it was about strategic alignment with where millions of Nigerians are already heading.

I tidied up ALL my obligations to the ADC before leaving. I ensured a clean transition. I served with honor while I was there.

I don't suffer incompetence or corruption quietly. Some call that confrontational. I call it accountability.

I've been told I'm "too direct," "too outspoken," "too uncompromising."

Nigeria didn't get into this mess because of people who spoke up too much. We got here because too many people stayed silent when they should have been shouting.

The Senate doesn't need more people who "get along." It needs people who get RESULTS.

🗳️The Campaign

The Federal Capital Territory Senate election is on Saturday, February 14, 2027.

Voter registration is ongoing. Make sure you're registered, know your polling unit, and mark your calendar!

I'm running for the Federal Capital Territory (FCT) Senate seat in the National Assembly.

The FCT Senator represents ALL residents across the six area councils: AMAC, Bwari, Gwagwalada, Kuje, Kwali, and Abaji — all 3.5 million of you.

The Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) is the political party I'm contesting under. It's the party Peter Obi chose as the vehicle for the movement toward competence-based, character-driven governance in Nigeria.

The NDC represents where millions of Nigerians — across tribes, religions, and regions — are placing their hope for 2027.

Money matters in elections, but it's not everything.

What we're building is something money can't buy: authentic grassroots support.

When 100,000 volunteers knock on doors, register voters, and share our message, that's more powerful than billboards. Look at Peter Obi's 2023 campaign — massively outspent by the establishment, yet created the largest grassroots movement in Nigeria's history.

Our currency is credibility, track record, and commitment to service. Those things can't be bought, only earned. And I've spent 21 years earning them.

Electoral fraud is real, and we're not naive about it. Our strategy is fourfold:

  1. Massive voter education — teaching people that their vote is worth more than any handout.
  2. 100,000 polling unit volunteers — monitoring the process and reporting irregularities in real-time.
  3. Civil society & international observer partnerships for transparency.
  4. Aggressive legal action — we will pursue EVERY documented case of fraud.

I'll continue fighting for citizens — my commitment doesn't depend on holding office. But we're not planning to lose.

The 100,000 volunteers we're training don't disappear on February 15, 2027. The accountability structures we're creating don't vanish. This is bigger than one election.

With your help, we won't have to worry about "what if we lose." We're going to shock them in 2027.

  4. Education — "No Child Left Behind"
  5. Maternal Health — "Pregnancy Is Not A Death Sentence"
  6. Land Rights — "They Can't Just Bulldoze Your Home"
  7. Transparency — "You Will See Where Every Kobo Goes"

🛡️Security & Safety

Multi-pronged approach:

  1. Enhanced community policing on all major routes
  2. Mandatory registration and GPS tracking of all commercial vehicles
  3. Undercover "one chance" task force targeting known hotspots
  4. Designated safe taxi ranks at major locations
  5. Partnership with ride-hailing companies for data sharing
  6. Quick-response teams with 5-minute target response time
Target: 70% reduction in "one chance" incidents within the first year.

Rural security strategy:

  1. Community policing partnerships with local vigilantes and traditional rulers
  2. Technology-driven surveillance in high-risk areas
  3. Quick-response teams stationed in each area council
  4. Intelligence gathering through trusted community networks
  5. Improved road access for security and emergency vehicles

No community should be too remote for protection.

🏘️Indigenous Rights

Legislative backing, not just promises. The Indigenous Rights & Land Protection Bill includes:

  1. Mandatory full compensation BEFORE any land acquisition
  2. Land Rights Tribunal with indigenous representation
  3. Community veto power over developments affecting ancestral lands
  4. Retroactive compensation for past land taken without payment
  5. Legal penalties for government officials who violate these rights
This will be LAW, not policy. Enforceable. Actionable. Justiciable. The Gbagyi, Koro, Bassa, and Gade people will finally get justice.
